## Executive Summary

Meedok-chat is a new customer-facing product designed to assist doctors and physicians in their daily clinical workflow. Its core capability is an AI-powered chatbot that helps medical professionals clarify prescriptions and validate diagnoses based on patient symptoms and medical history. The primary business problem is a cost and efficiency gap: consultation diagnosis times currently range from minutes to hours, creating unnecessary delays in patient care. The key outcome sought is a measurable reduction in consultation time and improved clarity of diagnostic results for the physician.

---

## Business Context

### Problem Statement

Currently, the medical consultation and diagnosis process is highly manual and time-consuming, ranging from minutes to hours depending on the complexity of the case. Doctors must review patient history, cross-reference symptoms, and validate diagnoses without AI-assisted support. Meedok-chat aims to reduce diagnosis times by synthesising patient symptoms and medical history into a fast, clear summary for the responsible physician, and by helping validate diagnoses through a conversational chatbot interface.

### Business Drivers

This product is being pursued now for internal efficiency and cost reduction reasons. No hard external deadline has been specified.
